# Middle-earth homepage expansion — v2

Give Codex this folder and ask it to execute CODEX-PROMPT.md. This is an ADD-ON to the first asset pack, not a replacement. It contains all seven new artworks from the mine/Bree/Hobbit request and the two dedicated thermostat states (seven total files: five earlier new pieces plus two thermostat pieces).

Original PNGs are preserved without edits or upscaling. Actual dimensions and SHA-256 checksums are in asset-manifest.json. These are native 1393–1916px-wide assets, NOT 4K masters. Use the dedicated individual thermostat assets instead of their smaller atlas equivalents. Some alpha images include translucent lighting halos; review against the actual surface. RGB scenic backdrops intentionally have no transparency.

Included: closed mine entrance, Lonely Mountain treasury interior, Bree/Prancing Pony noticeboard, Hobbit oak/ivy frame, six-icon home atlas, heating fireplace, cooling OPEN window. No open-door animation frames are supplied; use a short fade for the transition.

Existing header, original Frear art, earlier frames, and earlier email scroll are intentionally not duplicated. Preserve them. This pack contains artwork and instructions, not a deployed or tested site implementation.
